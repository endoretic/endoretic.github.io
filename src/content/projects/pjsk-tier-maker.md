A static tier-list maker for Project SEKAI songs.

Song metadata and jacket images are not bundled. They are loaded at runtime
from remote resources provided by Moesekai and Sekai.best.

The original source is released under the MIT licence. That licence does not
grant rights to Project SEKAI content, song metadata, jacket images, or the
other third-party resources fetched at runtime; those remain with their
respective rights holders and service providers.
